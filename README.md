### 🌱 How I Work

<p align="center"><strong>🍇 FIELD &nbsp;→&nbsp; 🧬 MEASURE &nbsp;→&nbsp; 💻 MODEL &nbsp;→&nbsp; 🌱 DECIDE</strong></p>

🌿 **Crop scientist:** I begin with the plant, the field pattern, and the biological question.

🧬 **Molecular scientist:** I measure the signal with RT-qPCR, digital PCR, RNA-seq, and metabolomics.

📊 **Data scientist:** I build reproducible pipelines, integrate evidence, test models, and communicate uncertainty.

🎯 **Translator:** I turn results into diagnostic markers, biological mechanisms, and crop-improvement questions.

### 🔬 What I Work On

🍇 **Grapevine–virus interactions** · Transcriptomic and metabolomic profiling across commercial-vineyard samples, seasons, cultivars, and maturity stages

🧬 **Molecular diagnostics** · RT-qPCR and digital PCR assay development, validation, and quality control for plant pathogens

📊 **Multi-omics data science** · Differential expression, pathway analysis, biomarker discovery, causal analysis, Bayesian inference, and machine learning

🧪 **Food safety and formulation** · Plant-derived nano-formulations, toxin control, release modeling, and mechanism-of-action studies
